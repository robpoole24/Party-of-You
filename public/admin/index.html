Processed Datasets — Election Intelligence

These datasets were processed from MIT Election Lab public data and are ready to load into the database. Upload them via the Bulk Datasets tab.

district_partisan_lean.csv
599 districts with lean index, label, and trend. Core metric for candidate recruitment.
competitive_districts.csv
276 districts within 12 points. 44 true toss-ups. The candidate recruitment shortlist.
district_intelligence.csv
Full district profiles with trend direction, competitiveness flags, and election history.
precinct_district_2024.csv
2024 actual results aggregated to congressional district — 4,166 districts across 47 states.
election_history.csv
37,000+ rows of cleaned results 1976–2024. House, Senate, President. Source for all calculations.
state_summary.csv
State-level roll-up: how many Safe D / Lean D / Toss-up / Lean R / Safe R districts per state.

Source: MIT Election Data + Science Lab — public federal data. No subscription required. electionlab.mit.edu/data
