🗃️ Get Your Voter File

State voter files are public record. Most states provide them free or for a small processing fee. You generally need to be a registered candidate to request one.

How to Request
  1. Register your campaign committee with your state
  2. Contact your Secretary of State's elections division
  3. Submit a voter data request form with your committee ID
  4. Pay any processing fee (usually $0-25)
  5. Upload the file here — we parse any format
What You'll Get
✓ Name, address, age for every registered voter
✓ Party registration (where available)
✓ Voting history (which elections, not who they voted for)
✓ Phone numbers (in some states)
✗ Email addresses (not in voter files)
