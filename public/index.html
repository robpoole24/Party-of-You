How It Works

From Address to Candidate
in Four Steps

Everything a new candidate needs to know, organized so you can act on it.

01
Find Your Races

Enter your address. We resolve it to your congressional, state, and local districts and pull every open or contested seat you're eligible to run for.

02
Know Your District

Demographics, partisan lean, current polling, issue priorities, and your incumbent's voting record and donor list — assembled from federal sources automatically.

03
Get on the Ballot

State-specific signature requirements, printable petition sheets, filing deadlines, and the exact office to contact. We track your signature count as you go.

04
Run Your Campaign

Website, email, events, volunteer lists, phone banking, mailer templates, and AI-powered ad creation — all in one free dashboard built for candidates who have no staff.

Why This Exists

The Deck Is Stacked.
We're Unrigging It.

The major parties provide their candidates with data, technology, consultants, and fundraising networks. Grassroots candidates get none of it. Until now.

🆓

Permanently Free

No subscription. No premium tier. No commission on your donations. Every tool is available to every candidate who agrees to our platform. Third-party data costs we share transparently — and once funded, stay funded for everyone.

🚫

No Corporate Money

We don't take it. You don't take it. Every donation to your campaign is a real person who chose to support you — not a PAC whose executives want something in return. That difference is the whole point.

📊

Real Data

The same category of intelligence that party-backed candidates pay consultants for — census demographics, live polling, FEC fundraising records, historical election results — assembled automatically from public federal sources.

🤝

Community First

Your campaign website doubles as a community resource hub through our Just Help integration. Find food banks, shelters, and assistance programs in your district. Run a volunteer day. Show up before Election Day.

🗂️

Your Data, Always

Your donor list, volunteer contacts, and campaign content belong to you. If you ever leave the platform for any reason, you receive a full export within 14 days. No hostage data. No vendor lock-in.

⚡

Built for Beginners

First time running? Good. This platform is designed for people who have never run for office and don't have a network of political consultants. Every step is explained. Every tool is documented.
